How a Round Works

  1. 1

    Choose in secret

    Choose one suit and a living opponent. Confirm to lock in, or pass. No time limit.

  2. 2

    Reveal together

    Once all living players confirm or pass, reveal cards and targets.

  3. 3

    Place new defenses

    New Spades can block damage this round.

  4. 4

    Resolve attacks together

    Add up incoming damage before blocking. Attacks still land if their player is eliminated this round.
